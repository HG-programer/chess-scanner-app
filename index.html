Privacy Policy for ChessSnap: AI Board Scanner

Effective Date: September 22, 2026
Last Updated: September 22, 2026

Privacy Highlights

  • No User Accounts: No sign-up, email, phone, or name is ever requested.
  • On-Device Camera Vision: Real-time frame processing for chessboard detection runs entirely locally in RAM. No photos or video feeds are transmitted to remote servers.
  • Zero Audio Recording: Microphone permission (RECORD_AUDIO) is explicitly disabled and removed.
  • Zero Storage Permissions: No access requested to external storage, photos, or media files.

1. Permissions Used and Data Handling

A. Camera (android.permission.CAMERA)

Required to scan physical chessboards, screens, and book diagrams. The camera feed is processed in real time by on-device computer vision algorithms to convert square positions into standard chess Forsyth–Edwards Notation (FEN). Camera frames are never stored or transmitted.

B. Internet Access (android.permission.INTERNET)

Used to query public opening databases (such as the Lichess Cloud API), deliver non-intrusive advertisements via Google Mobile Ads, and handle subscription purchases via Google Play Billing.

2. Advertising (Google Mobile Ads)

The app displays banner ads and optional user-initiated rewarded video ads to unlock Pro features. We implement Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK for GDPR, UK GDPR, and CCPA compliance. Advertising identifiers are handled according to Google Play Developer Program Policies.

3. Subscriptions & In-App Purchases

All in-app purchases and subscriptions are processed securely through Google Play In-App Billing (Billing Library 9). ChessSnap never accesses or stores payment information.
